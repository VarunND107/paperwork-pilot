    How it works

    Every step knows what it needs from the others.

    Official information exists, but it sits on dozens of sites and never tells you the sequence. Paperwork Pilot stores each step as a link in a chain, so the order is worked out for you.

    The order holds up

    Each step lists what it needs and what it produces. The bank claim waits for both the certified copies and the legal heir certificate, so you never queue for it too early.

    • Death certificate
    • Aadhaar of heirs
    • Bank passbook
    • Self-attested copy

    One document list for the whole route

    Whatever one step produces is ticked for you when you finish it. What is left is what you still have to collect.

    Deadlines that count down

    A death is registered within 21 days. Paperwork Pilot counts from the date of the event and reminds you before the window closes.

    Tips from people who stood in that queue

    Short notes per office, moderated and dated, and easy to flag when they go stale.
